Our Story

Fifteen Years on the Same Shoreline

RR Resort opened in 2010 with twelve rooms and one promise: never let the sea out of sight. What began as a family-run guesthouse has grown into a coastal retreat, without losing the unhurried, personal service it started with.

Mission & Vision

We exist to slow people down — through honest hospitality, thoughtful design, and a deep respect for the coastline we call home. Our vision is a resort that feels less like a stay, and more like a place you return to.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in is subject to availability.

Is airport pickup available?+

Yes, we arrange pickup and drop from the nearest airport and railway station on request — just mention it in your booking.

Do you allow pets?+

Well-behaved pets are welcome in our Family Villas with prior notice. Other room types are pet-free.

What is the cancellation policy?+

Free cancellation up to 7 days before check-in. Cancellations within 7 days are subject to a one-night charge.
